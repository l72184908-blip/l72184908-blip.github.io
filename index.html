About

About me

I’m Rizbi Khan — a student, and a developer and designer in one. Development and design aren’t two separate jobs for me: the way Moon-Chat looks, feels and protects its users all came out of the same head.

Moon-Chat is built around one idea: a conversation should stay a conversation. That is why screenshots and screen recording are blocked, a shared photo never saves to the other person’s phone, and a sent message cannot be deleted.

Building new things, designing them, and understanding them deeply — that is my work. If you have an idea, or want to know more about Moon-Chat, get in touch.
